Mossglen

A cozy village, rebuilt a little at a time. Every version below was judged before it was kept. Newest first — open two of them side by side.

v003 2026-08-16 20/25

The house, repainted: one room seen straight on, a mat by the door, and a shelf you can see while you decide what goes on it. The neighbours have faces now, and opinions about your furniture.

 v002 2026-08-16 19/25

The village board. Six things to build — a bench, a lantern, stepping stones — each one somebody's idea, each one paid for out of what you have gathered. Then the green, which never fills up.

 v001 2026-08-16 16/25

The clearing, the well, and three neighbours: gather things, furnish your house with them, give them to whoever was hoping for one, and watch the village open up.
Each version keeps its own save. Opening a new one never disturbs an old one.
