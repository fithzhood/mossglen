**Condition 2 — `worstAbsoluteRepetition` must come down from 55; any rise is automatic rejection. NOT MET: it is 124, a 125% rise. I rule the condition miscalibrated and do not fire the grind veto. The rejection below rests on the ratchet, not on this.**

I am not taking the developer's reasoning on trust; here is the arithmetic from the file.

*The metric measures the wrong thing.* `worstAbsoluteRepetition` compares raw counts across windows that differ in length by two orders of magnitude — `stage:2` holds 8 progress actions, `grove:750` holds 736. Normalised, the worst window in v004 is **less** concentrated than v003's: 124/736 = 0.168 against 55/279 = 0.197. All 88 flagged entries sit between 0.10 and 0.20 shareOfWindow; not one exceeds 0.20; `monotonousWindows` is empty. Per unit of play time the number moved the *other* way: seed 2024's worst window spans days 191→208, so 124 moss pulls over 17 in-game days is 7.3/day, against v003's 55 over ~7 days at 7.9/day. The metric rose 125% while the quantity it exists to measure fell slightly.

*The two conditions the predecessor set are mutually exclusive.* Backlog item 1 (scored 5/5, named as the single most important weakness) demanded "a threshold at least every 20 in-game days out to day 200." Backlog item 2 demanded `worstAbsoluteRepetition` below 55 against a fixed `grindThreshold: 15`. The player performs ~43 progress actions per in-game day in the back half (736 actions / 17 days). Twenty in-game days is therefore ~860 progress actions, and for no single action to exceed 55 in an 860-action window the most common action must be under 6.4% of everything the player does — with a handful of gathering spots and action types, that is arithmetically impossible. The developer could satisfy item 1 or item 2, not both, and chose the one the predecessor scored 5/5. That was the right choice. The record supports the good-faith account: 412 → 144 → 124 via denser milestones, and a spot-density experiment reverted the same cycle after it pushed `itemsLeftUnusedAtEnd` to 7,744 and dialogue reach to 87.7%.

*Overruling a predecessor's pre-commitment is a cost I am accepting deliberately.* A binding condition that can only be met by undoing the version's headline fix is not a standard, it is a trap, and enforcing it would teach this loop that the way to pass is to keep the game short. I am on record: **the pre-commitment was wrong and I am voiding it.**

The gate becomes **shareOfWindow: no milestone window may show any single action above 0.20.** v004's maximum is 0.19 (`built:steppingstones`, seed 888001; and the worst grove window is also 0.19), so this is hold-the-line, and it is immune to window length.
