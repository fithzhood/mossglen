# Scorecard — v001

## Scores
A. Interlock — 3/5 — All four pillars are genuinely wired together (gathered items feed the Well and villager gifts, which advance village stages, which open the pondpath and hollow and their new items), but the whole circle completes by in-game day 3 and then every arrow goes dead.
B. Systemic health — 2/5 — Zero crashes, zero softlocks and full item/area/villager reachability sit on top of an economy the metrics themselves flag as broken: `bloomHasNoSinkAfterFinalStage: true`, overspill 277.8x, 3,958.6 items unused at end of run.
C. Visual charm — 3/5 — A coherent forest palette and genuinely excellent phone readability, with villagers and a duck that visibly move between morning and evening, undercut by a dashed placeholder rectangle rendered inside the rug, the word "out" baked onto the door, and a home drawn in four different projections at once.
D. Voice — 4/5 — Three distinguishable, warm, slightly odd people with lines as good as "Thank you — I mean that in the slow way, not the quick one", let down by a single Bodkin line firing three times in a fourteen-line sample.
E. Session feel — 4/5 — All five seeds end three minutes on a find, a line, a village change and something placed, but all five also end at stage 3 of 4, so the first session spends most of the game.
**Total: 16/25**

## Verdict
ACCEPTED. No veto fires, the pillar circle demonstrably closes rather than merely being asserted, and the writing is better than this genre usually manages. But the acceptance is thin: the game's own metrics file raises `bloomHasNoSinkAfterFinalStage: true` and an overspill factor of 277.8, and the milestone table shows the entire content arc finishing on in-game day 3 of a 300-day simulation. v002 must move the economy and the pacing, or v001's 16 will have been the high-water mark.

## The single most important weakness
The game is over in about twelve real minutes and then keeps running for 297 more in-game days with nothing left to give. `perSeed[0].pacing.dayReached` shows every milestone in the game — all 10 items, all 3 areas, all 3 villagers, `home:full`, and `stage:4` — first occurring on in-game days 1 to 3; `stage:4` lands on day 2 in four of five seeds. At the configured 4 real minutes per in-game day that is roughly 8 to 12 real minutes of play. `home:full` on day 1 is the sharpest edge of it: decorating, the pillar whose job is to absorb what gathering produces, saturates before the second sunrise, after which gathering feeds nothing. The economy shows exactly that shape — `bloomOverspillFactor: 277.8` against a `finalStageBloomCost` of 140, `itemsLeftUnusedAtEnd: 3958.6`, and glass bead, the rarest and best-drawn find in the bag, running 769 inflow against 25 outflow across the run. The three-minute metrics hide this rather than contradict it: every seed reports `pleasantOutcome: true`, and every seed also reports `stageAtEnd: 3`, so the delightful first session is spending three of the game's four stages. Two supporting details confirm the absorbing end of the loop is the weak one: `4-home-interior.png` holds only six placed objects, and the single dialogue node no seed ever reaches, `marla.home.feather`, is a villager reacting to a home decoration. The fix is not more content to gather — it is somewhere for the back half of the circle to put what is already being gathered.

## New backlog candidates

1. **Post-stage-4 village projects that consume bloom and bulk materials on repeat** — 5/5 — pillars 1, 2, 4 (and 3 when a villager proposes one). Add village-scale builds that unlock *after* the final stage and can be commissioned repeatedly: a bridge, a bench, a lantern path, each costing hundreds of moss/acorn/pebble plus bloom, each placing a visible object on the shared village map. Target `bloomOverspillFactor` under 5 and `itemsLeftUnusedAtEnd` under 400 across a 300-day run. This is the item that stops the game dying at minute twelve.

2. **Stretch the unlock arc from 3 in-game days to about 30** — 5/5 — pillars 1, 4. Every seed reaches `stage:4` on day 2 or 3 and `home:full` on day 1. Gate the later areas and the later items (reed, feather, glass bead) behind stages that require small contributions across several days rather than one burst, so that `dayReached` still shows first-time milestones on days 4 through 30. Do this by spreading gates over time, not by raising costs: `gather:hollowtree` already sits at 13 required repetitions against a grind threshold of 15.

3. **Make the decorating screen show what is being decorated, and give villagers placed items to react to** — 4/5 — pillars 2, 3. In `5-decorating.png` the sheet titled "On the corner" covers the entire floor, so the player picks blind, with no view of the target spot, what is already there, or a preview. Replace the full-height grid with a single pinned scrollable row, highlight the target spot in the room above it, and ghost the selected item into place before confirm. Pair it with reaction lines keyed to specific placed items: the sample contains exactly one such line (Pim, day 35: "That's exactly the one. The shelf's level.") and the game's only dead dialogue node, `marla.home.feather`, is precisely this kind of line.

4. **Redraw the home interior: kill the placeholder rug, the baked-in "out", and the mixed projections** — 4/5 — pillars 2, 3. In `4-home-interior.png` the rug is a flat lavender slab with a dashed white rectangle rendered inside it during normal play — it reads unmistakably as a debug drop-target — and it is the only untextured, un-outlined, off-palette object in the build. The door carries "out" in large white sans-serif baked onto its texture. The window and wall picture are flat green rectangles with a lone orange square. Wall shelves and window are drawn in elevation, bed and rug top-down, table in side view with a moss ball resting on it as if seen from above. Commit the room to one projection, give the rug a woven pixel texture inside the existing green/brown/cream ramp, and replace the "out" text with a mat or a step.

5. **Give Bodkin a real line pool, keyed to what the player is carrying** — 3/5 — pillars 3, 1. "I dig at night as well. It's the same dark, so it's no extra trouble." fires on days 17, 23 and 27 — three of Bodkin's four appearances in a fourteen-line sample are the same sentence, while Marla's six lines are all distinct. Write him 8 to 10 more day and night lines and key several to the contents of the bag, so his repeat rate over a 300-day run falls to Marla's. His conversation portrait in `3-conversation.png` is also the weakest sprite in the game — an upscaled world sprite with a pink smear where the face should be and no readable eyes.
